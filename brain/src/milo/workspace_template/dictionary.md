# Dictionary

Words Milo should get right when you dictate. When you fix a word he pasted, he learns the fix here by himself.
You can add, change or delete lines too.

## Words
Names and terms Whisper should spell right, one per line, like `- Milo`.

## Fixes
`- heard -> meant`: what Whisper wrote, and what you meant. Milo puts the meant words in before he pastes.
A fix he learned says how often he saw it. He uses it after one fix if it's a name ("Cloud Code -> Claude Code"),
otherwise from the second. A fix you write without "(seen N)" always counts.
